FIVE DIRECTIONS
01 A note from the builder / candid founder
02 Show the work / professional workflow
03 Start with your eye / welcoming tutorial
04 The final final club / playful editing pain
05 A point of view / cinematic editorial

Each direction contains a creative/storyboard board (PNG + SVG), a social image
(PNG + SVG) and a vertical MP4 motion draft. Boards are standalone creative
direction layouts, not native Looksy documents. Editable SVGs embed fonts and art.

01–03 videos are animatics. Labels identify the founder and real app footage
still to record. 04–05 are graphic motion drafts with no founder filming needed.
All five have an original synthesized audio bed and onscreen copy, no voiceover.
The exact rendered scripts run 18 seconds; separate live-action master scripts
run 24 seconds. These are different versions, not a duration discrepancy.

The tracker starts with 15 delivered drafts, zero creative approvals and all
production tasks pending. Update task completion and review cells when reviewed.
No posts have been published. No ad spend or publication date is committed.
Campaign uses requested LOOKSi spelling; application files remain unchanged.

ARTWORK AND FONTS
The optical artwork is AI generated via the built-in image_gen tool; full prompt
and provenance are in artwork/provenance.json. It is illustrative brand art and
does not demonstrate an image edited in Looksi. The waveform is original
sample-free synthesis; audio/provenance.json describes it. Fonts are bundled
with their OFL licenses under artwork/fonts/. No reference campaign media copied.
