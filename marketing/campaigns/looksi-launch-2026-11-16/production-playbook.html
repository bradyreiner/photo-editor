What is ready to review

Every direction has a 1920×1080 creative/storyboard PNG, an editable SVG board, a 1080×1350 social PNG, an editable SVG image and an 18-second 1080×1920 H.264/AAC video. There are five of each format. SVGs include their fonts and any artwork. Boards explain direction and production; they are presentation/storyboard boards, rather than native Looksy application documents.

Videos 01–03 are production animatics: type, timing, an original sound bed and explicit labels for the live-action or app footage still required. Videos 04–05 are complete graphic motion drafts for creative review, with no founder filming requirement. All five include onscreen copy and a sample-free synthesized sound bed, with no spoken voice. No generated image is represented as a Looksi editing result.

The 18-second scripts are in shot-timing.json. The briefs also contain longer capture scripts for live-action production; their duration is a separate version. Filmed versions may need a different cadence to make the controls readable. Preserve the captured frame rate when finishing the real demo rather than stretching the present animatic to imply task speed.

Product capture that earns the claim

02 / The professional proof

Select an owned shoot with one still and one short video. Record the build identifier and media properties. Show the original, one deliberate photo decision, one timeline action, the export operation and the opened exported result. Keep the raw recording. The final film may crop panels for readability; disclose any time compression.

03 / The first edit

Pick one owned photo with a clear learning opportunity. Show a crop and one light adjustment at a pace someone can follow, compare with the original, then save/export and open the result. Use the actual control names visible in the capture. A short social cut should lead to a longer walkthrough if the steps need more time.

For 01, reuse a short, representative excerpt from the captured workflow after checking it still matches the founder’s words. Clear private file paths and personal information before recording. Existing marketing fixture captures remain internal development material. Keep source media, the continuous recording, final project and exported files together, so the evidence can be revisited.

Do not add app UI to the playful or editorial versions merely to fill space. If a future 04 version promises resolution of revision chaos, record the exact feature that resolves it first. The current joke expresses familiarity with the problem and an ambition to build a better experience; it makes no version-management claim.

Learn from the launch

Use one campaign/format/source tag per post, for example utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=looksi_build&utm_content=01_founder_video_a when a working destination exists. For posts with no clickable link, keep the post URL and profile visits, and identify attribution as indirect. Do not treat all profile traffic as a demo start.

Define a qualified visit as an observed visit to a genuine demo or feedback destination from someone in the intended editing audience; count it only where your collection method can establish it. Define useful feedback as a concrete workflow, problem or repeatable test, and retain a short anonymized note. Where qualification cannot be determined, use raw visits plus qualitative notes instead.

Record counts in the tracker after the same seven-day window. Keep paid and organic separate. Compare style variants within the same platform and format before comparing across channels. Report saves/reach or shares/reach only where both numerator and denominator are available with matching definitions; leave missing measurements blank. A 3-second view is not a finished demo, and no small organic sample here establishes statistical superiority.

If reach is low, document what people actually said and improve the next post. If a hook earns attention but no relevant response, clarify what the tool is and who it is for. If viewers ask about a feature, answer with a real demonstration or an accurate development status. Choose the next investment from the quality of these signals, not a predeclared winner.
