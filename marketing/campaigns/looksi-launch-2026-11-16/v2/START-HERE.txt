START WITH creative-direction-book.pdf.
It is a 20-page visual direction book containing the strategy, five campaign
platforms, actual art/storyboards, matching copy, proof scope and production plan.
Open index.html manually for the local studio with films, carousels and captions.

WHAT IS DELIVERED
Five campaign routes, each with a cover image, storyboard board and finished
primary film. Primary films run 32/42/33/24/36 seconds. Every route has two alternate
openings, giving 15 full film versions, with 15 matching silent masters.
There are five rendered Instagram carousels, 31 slides total.
Editable live-text SVGs and portable outlined vector masters accompany the art.
Fonts and their OFL licenses are included. Canonical filenames are consistent:
campaignXX/images/cover.png; campaignXX/storyboard/board.png;
campaignXX/videos/hero.mp4 (and hero-silent.mp4, hook-b/c variants).

01 / I wanted to make this. — a finished builder letter with actual work.

03 / Keep the gold. Keep the quiet blues. — a specific photo recipe breakdown.
04 / The last tweak. Again. — an original animated story about creative revision.

MEDIA AND COLOR
New films use original sample-free scores. Archived source audio is not used
in those films. Sound-on masters use AAC 48 kHz; all final streams have explicit
limited-range BT.709 matrix/primaries/transfer metadata. Original RGB frames were
actually converted before encoding; tags alone were not treated as conversion.
Delivered PNGs carry sRGB profiles. This is production verification, not an
independent color-accuracy or display qualification.
