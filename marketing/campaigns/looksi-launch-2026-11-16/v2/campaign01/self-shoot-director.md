# Optional founder performance / campaign01

The finished 32 second builder letter already stands alone. This is a future 40 second face-led version and a 24 second cutdown plan, not missing material for that film. Allow 25 minutes. These are production estimates, not recorded time.

## Set

Use an eye-level phone/camera, a window or soft light 45 degrees to one side, and Brady’s actual workspace. A chair, quiet background and visible work are enough. Keep the lens close to eye line and record a ten-second test for room sound. A small microphone helps more than a second light. Record vertical with a little headroom and enough space below the face for captions; keep face and hands away from the far right and bottom platform overlays.

Do not close or restart the editor, discard unsaved work or rearrange an active project for a background shot. Use an isolated campaign document for any future screen action. The older native footage can still be used, with the archive date visible.

## Shoot order

1. Full 40 second read twice: once direct and quiet; once a little more conversational. Leave a beat between sentences.
2. Three short openings: “I wanted to make this.” / “I built a tool. Bring a real project.” / “What should this editor handle?”
3. Three endings: “What would you want to test?” / “Tell me what you make.” / “Give me a real project to put it through.”
4. Five seconds each: looking at an actual image; returning to the lens; hand resting near the keyboard. These are connective observations, not fake app actions.
5. Ten seconds of quiet room tone. Record the full words once more as clean audio if the camera take sounds distant.
