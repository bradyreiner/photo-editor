# LOOKSi / campaign strategy and execution register

The marketing job is to earn useful curiosity about a photo and video editor a person built for both professional work and personal creative projects. The promise is deliberate creative control and a considered way through the work. Specific output and native operation evidence support specific claims; “better” remains the maker’s ambition until the corresponding improvement is demonstrated.

## What changed after the first pass

The first pass supplied five voices but used interchangeable title-card films, generic benefits and capture placeholders. V2 starts with real source material, gives each route a different visual object and story, and uses actual native exports. The founder route is now a finished letter rather than an empty talking-head slot. The humor route has an original poster that becomes a character. The learner route teaches a particular image decision. The professional route is a file-based casebook with dates and outputs. The manifesto ties visual taste to a real product moment.

## Audience priorities

Working photographers and hybrid editors need a reason to inspect the file, its treatment and its delivery. Lead them to 02 and the actual exported examples. Describe one project rather than claiming reliability for every codec, camera or client deadline. The useful response is a concrete test requirement, a handoff question or someone repeating a demonstrated task.

People who make photographs for themselves need an achievable creative decision, a reason for it and enough room to look. Lead them to 03. The Nepal lesson asks what should stay warm and what should stay quiet, then identifies three combined choices. It does not call a recipe universal. Useful feedback includes a learning question or an actual first-edit result.

Design-conscious photographers, filmmakers and makers may first care about the point of view. 01 introduces the builder; 05 expresses taste with photographs, genuine native action footage and actual output. Those routes should connect to 02/03 so the visual world has product substance. 04 opens a lighter conversation about craft without pretending that a graphic joke proves a feature.

## Message hierarchy

1. Identify the maker and the medium: “I’m building LOOKSi, a photo and video editor.”
2. Give the audience a concrete project or recognizable creative decision.
3. Show actual work at a scale a person can inspect.
4. State current source/recording scope where it affects interpretation.
5. Ask one specific question about their work.

## Reasons to believe and proof boundaries

The fresh photo example is an actual native before/chosen export pair from the same 1800 × 1200 JPEG source. The chosen treatment combines 5900 K, contrast +12% and exposure +0.15 EV. Neither result is a Python brightness illustration. The first attempted export did not change pixels and is explicitly excluded from the comparison.

The board casebook contains six actual native 1920 × 1536 exports from NEPAL / NEAR & FAR, dated 16 November. The delivery receipt proves the exported image files; it does not, on its own, prove every piece of text was authored through the current visible UI.

The native Timeline output is a 12-second, 360-frame, 1920 × 1080 / 30 fps review excerpt observed on 2 November. Its exact SHA-256 matches its native receipt. It is finished motion-design output, rather than a recording of current Timeline manipulation or evidence for a complete 298-second delivery. The casebook deliberately says the pieces were made across sessions.

Native tone-curve recordings in 01 and 05 are archived development footage from 25 October and are dated on screen. Their controls and photograph are real; no new app interface is fabricated. The current editor’s external MCP is disabled. During this pass a separate workshop photo was imported and exported through the native UI; the original Nepal board documents were not edited or closed. A later Timeline setup made the window inaccessible while its process remained running. No forced restart, policy change or fabricated recovery is included.

## Channel-native deployment

Instagram: publish original native exports with the matching caption and alt text, checking the current placement crop. Use the image carousel to carry one complete small idea. A Reel should show the work in its opening, then give it a recognizable story. Direct Instagram launch-post inspection was limited; the research labels those adaptations.

Reddit: use a builder account, disclose the relationship in the first sentence, choose one relevant community or release thread that permits the post, and make the body useful without requiring a click. The five drafted bodies are alternatives for different contexts, rather than a cross-post blast. Verify the actual rules immediately before publishing. No message or post was sent.

YouTube/LinkedIn: use the professional casebook or the longer optional proof demonstrations when viewers need time to inspect controls and outputs. Captions should describe exactly what the selected cut shows. A six-board graphic set or a motion review excerpt should not be presented as one fresh end-to-end recording.

After creative, media and destination review, introduce the builder with 01 on D0. Follow with the specific output casebook 02 on D+2, the approachable recipe 03 on D+4, the craft joke 04 on D+7 and the photographic manifesto 05 on D+10. D+14 is an early review of observations; each post’s seven-day actuals mature on its own date. These are relative planning dates, not scheduled posts. Finished pieces can be reviewed independently; optional founder shoots do not block the existing letter film.

## Experiments and learning

Each route has a specific audience job and hook options. Keep the body, format, caption and audience as constant as practical when comparing an opening. Organic posting is exploratory evidence, not a randomized causal experiment. Do not publish duplicate promotions to satisfy a test design.

Use a separate source/format/variant tag. Record the post URL, platform, publication time, observation window, exact denominator definitions and actual counts. Keep missing values blank and distinguish zero from unavailable. Track qualified demo visits only where the tracking method can establish them. Keep an anonymized note for useful feedback so a conversation is not reduced to a vanity count.

For 01, look for concrete project briefs. For 02, look for relevant technical questions or attempted repetitions. For 03, look for learning questions, saves and first-edit reports. For 04, distinguish joke-only engagement from actual product interest. For 05, distinguish a brief aesthetic impression from someone asking to see the work. Compare within a channel and format, and report small samples as observations. No performance benchmarks, customer numbers, budget, reach or revenue have been invented.

## Production decisions

The current five primary films need creative approval and publication checks, with no founder impersonation or synthetic voice. A self-shot founder film is a separate optional version with a director script. Longer current-build photo/board walkthroughs are optional evidence upgrades; the existing files remain honestly scoped review material. Role labels in the tracker are hats a person may fill, rather than hired vendors. Hours are planning estimates.

Pricing, checkout and commercial terms remain unapproved in release/product.json. Development conversation posts can invite feedback without advertising purchase availability. Sales or “available now” language needs a separately verified release and access journey. The current campaigns use feedback invitations.
