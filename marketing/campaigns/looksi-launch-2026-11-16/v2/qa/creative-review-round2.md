# Creative director review / round 2

16 November 2026 · independent review of delivered 02/03/05 boards, closed film snapshots and the reconciled central copy

## Direction and quality decision

V2 now has a credible creative system. The professional route has a real project object and three forms of native output. The learner route has a particular photograph, a visual intention and actual treatment values. The editorial route lets photographs carry the feeling, then connects them to a real product moment. Those are meaningful improvements in persuasion, not simply different type treatments.

The primary remaining P0 is reconciliation: some canonical claims still describe the abandoned capture concepts despite the captions and films being updated. Fix those fields before treating the central register as the source of truth. The most useful P1 work is specific and bounded: enlarge the actual teaching, preserve the native board compositions in the montage, clean consumer copy spacing and clarify the archived motion example's inner ad text.

The creative system should be judged as a finished set of review assets with accurately scoped production plans. It should not claim that the delivered films are five newly captured end-to-end demonstrations. They are a builder letter, output casebook, recipe breakdown, original graphic joke and photographic editorial film. That mix is consistent with the user's request for some animated work and some separately tracked human filming.

## P1 / improve the actual creative, not its paperwork

### 02 / the casebook should make three forms immediately visible

The current cover's photo, native-motion still and board composition communicate the proposition well. The hero opening sampled at 1.5 seconds shows the photo and a board inset, with motion represented only by a word in the label. Add a small native motion still to that first composition or reveal the three actual forms sequentially in the opening. This is not a claim that a product operation happened in three seconds; it simply fulfills the three-form visual promise before the viewer has to wait eleven seconds for the motion section.

The six-board grid is a useful overview. It is not large enough to inspect the fine design or typography at phone width. That is acceptable in an introductory hero if the companion carousel or output links make the complete files easy to open. Do not use “actual files to inspect” as a slogan without a direct and clear route to those files in the review/download package.

The archived native motion example visibly includes the old ad text **“One shoot. Every deliverable.”** and a camera-RAW line. Those words are legible in the phone sample even though the outer caption correctly identifies a review export. Their meaning can muddy the new JPEG/across-sessions casebook. Prefer selected prism/brand-only moments from the same genuine native artifact, or make its status as archived motion-design example unambiguous. Do not turn the inner ad into proof of RAW processing or universal delivery capability.

The final invitation occupies nine of the 42 seconds. That is a long hold for essentially one image and a question. A small purposeful progression—image to all three forms, then question—would sustain the ending without adding noise. A shorter cutdown is also reasonable. This is a craft improvement, not a conversion-rate claim.

### 03 / make the learning as visible as the photograph

The warm/cool intention is now source-specific and worth teaching. The paper treatment, serif headline and restrained blue annotations feel welcoming, while preserving the dignity of the photograph. The same-source comparison is appropriately subtle rather than a theatrical fake transformation.

The strongest remaining weakness is that the actual teaching is the smallest essential content. The ingredient labels are approximately 31 px and values 34 px in a 1080-wide master—about 10–11 pixels when viewed at 360-wide phone scale. They can be deciphered, but they do not receive the emphasis their job deserves.

Use the eight-second recipe section to make the three choices legible and meaningful. For example:

- **Temperature / 5900 K / a warmer direction**
- **Contrast / +12% / clearer separation**
- **Exposure / +0.15 EV / a modest lift**

Those are descriptions of the combined recipe, not isolated response claims. The source already defines rationale notes but does not draw them. Show one large choice at a time beside the held endpoint, or enlarge the three-row block with a clear purpose column. Keep the combined-treatment qualification in the comparison beat and caption.

The blue leader labeled “Keep this warmth” currently ends in empty sky above the lit ridge. Point it at the actual warm summit/ridge. A teaching annotation should direct the eye precisely; this is a small fix with more value than another general adjective.

The recipe beat, full-image comparison and clean endpoint hold should remain. Do not add invented control animation to make this look like a native tutorial. It is a useful endpoint breakdown; the optional capture plan can later show the actual gestures.

### 05 / preserve the compositions being celebrated

This is the strongest atmosphere of the three: the warm flowers, mountain layers, serif rhythm and quiet optical signature form a coherent point of view. The product moment gives the editorial language substance. It now feels related to the editor rather than an isolated optical logo film.

The board montage currently fills tall slots with landscape 5:4 board exports. This chops important headlines into fragments—“EAR/FAR” and partial “THE D…/BETW…” are visible in the sample. Cropping can be expressive, but here it undermines the line about giving the work somewhere to live and makes designed outputs look carelessly framed.

Use `contain=True`, preserve the native 5:4 board ratio, or deliberately reveal each full board before moving through details. A pan across complete board compositions can still have rhythm. Do not demonstrate a finished layout by accidentally mutilating its words. Apply the same care to the board inset in 02's later collage.

The real tone-curve footage is displayed as a whole interface at phone scale. Its controls are not readable in detail, but this route is an editorial introduction rather than a tutorial, so that is acceptable. The image, curve gesture and date should be understandable; anyone wanting exact controls should be directed to a separate proof piece. A closer crop is an optional enhancement, not a reason to fabricate a new UI state.

## P1 / finish the public copy

Consumer captions need ordinary spacing and typography before export to the final copybook. Examples in the reviewed snapshot include `exports:16November`, `excerpt:2November` and `chose5900K,contrast+12%,and exposure+0.15EV`. These read like raw production variables.

Use **“Photo and board exports: 16 November.”**, **“Native Timeline review excerpt: 2 November.”**, and **“For this photograph I chose 5900 K, contrast +12%, and exposure +0.15 EV.”** Preserve correct dates and source scope, but make them readable sentences.

The 01 Reddit body remains substantially longer than the other founder-style examples need. The first useful artifact description appears after several paragraphs of philosophy. A tighter current version can begin: **“I'm Brady, and I'm building LOOKSi for photo and video editing. This letter brings together an archived tone-curve recording and real image/board outputs from a Nepal project. Here's the kind of creative work I want the tool to support.”** Then one concrete goal, the question and the necessary date note. This is a P1 editorial recommendation; a long thoughtful post is not inherently wrong.

The new cover eyebrows **“A PHOTO RECIPE TO TRY”** and **“ON LIGHT, COLOR AND MAKING”** address the subject more naturally than the earlier creative-direction labels. The code was patched, but the viewed boards/covers still included earlier labels. Recheck the regenerated exports before calling that change delivered. The internal storyboard's role label may remain a director's description; consumer artwork should use the intended public line.

## Optional preferences / do not confuse with errors

- The 02 green accent and 03/05 blue accent are coherent within the additive RGB system. Choosing a different accent is taste, not a defect.
- The same Nepal image appearing across several campaigns creates a recognizable project bank. It does not require sourcing an unrelated shoot just to manufacture variety.
- A founder letter without a face can be personal and complete. It does not need synthetic likeness, a rushed camera session or an apology for being typographic.
- The restrained before/after is credible. Do not exaggerate the treatment to make a wipe more dramatic.
- The 05 serif voice can remain slow and editorial. Not every film needs the pace or sound of 04's graphic joke.
