# Creative director review / round 1

16 November 2026 · independent review of the V2 working package

## Decision

The strategy is materially stronger than V1: each route has an audience situation, a useful creative role and an evidence requirement. Campaign 04 is now a designed piece with an original visual object, deliberate comic scenes and a readable cover. The package still needs tighter alignment between the central specification, actual files and current native evidence before it can be presented as an agency-level campaign system.

The next priority is not more prose. It is a clear demonstration object for 01–03 and 05, one canonical description of 04, and mobile review of the actual rendered cuts. The strongest current product artifact is the newly verified Nepal image treatment. Use that honestly and build around its real visual difference.

This is a production review, not a finding that all five campaigns or all product workflows are finished. No other contributor's files were changed by this review.

## Campaign 04 / what works and what still needs direction

The cover is the strongest designed visual reviewed so far. It has hierarchy, an original cream poster that can recur, a graphite background, restrained RGB and a red handwritten correction. The 360-pixel preview retains the main title, poster and question. The serif/sans contrast gives it personality without importing a new brand palette.

The decoded contact sheet shows a real scene progression: declaration, alignment negotiation, changing crop, alternatives, pause, relapse and builder invitation. This is a substantive improvement over equal-duration typography cards. The local provenance correctly says the poster is campaign artwork and not Looksi output. Keep that distinction.

The comedy remains more evident in the written premise than in every sampled frame. A literal one-output-pixel move becomes approximately one-third of a screen pixel when a 1080-wide master is viewed at 360 pixels. The word, readout or reaction must carry the joke. Do not expect the viewer to perceive the actual displacement. A visible overshoot followed by the absurdly tiny correction is a stronger visual setup than microscopic motion alone.

The six alternatives are deliberately similar, which can be funny. Their differences will not be inspectable on a phone. The line **“Yes, they're different.”** therefore needs a clean, readable hold; it begins late in the three-second alternatives scene. Review its actual dwell time and consider giving the line a little more room instead of adding another illustration. The audience should have time to recognize the premise before the selected option enlarges.

The current category introduction arrives in the ending, while the opening header is tiny. That is acceptable for a creator-process film among followers, but weaker for a new audience trying to recognize a photo/video editor. Consider a readable early category cue—part of the graphic, not a paragraph—if the cut will be used for discovery.

The six-second ending contains maker disclosure, wordmark, category, care statement and CTA. The hierarchy is legible in the supplied samples, but this is one quarter of the film. A shorter discovery cut could retain the joke and one clear question while preserving the existing 24-second master for the richer version. This is an optional cutdown, not a requirement to destroy the current piece.

Do not add footage of controls merely to make the graphic film look more credible. Its credibility comes from being honest about what it is. Let 02 and 03 carry the product proof, and let a real link or adjacent post connect interested viewers to that proof.

## Copy and performance direction

The current central Reddit bodies run roughly 290–320 words each; their preview versions are about 327–339 words. Length alone is not a violation, but the five posts share a reflective essay structure. That makes the voices less distinct and delays the tangible artifact.

For the first founder post, use approximately: maker disclosure → exact attached project → one current limitation → task question. For the professional post, lead with source and delivered files. For teaching, include the actual useful steps inside the post. For humor, keep the short scene and question; the local campaign04 caption is closer to the right length than the central 290-word narrative. For editorial, give the film breathing room with a short category/status caption. Longer process essays can follow genuine questions.

A common vague phrase such as “the decisions behind the work” should earn its place through a nearby example. Here the example is concrete: keep the peak's gold while retaining the cooler shadow layers, try the treatment, inspect the delivered PNG. That is more distinctive than repeating “care,” “room” and “point of view” across five introductions.

Two scripts need immediate voice-over tightening:

- 01, 30–35 seconds: 18 words in five seconds, roughly 216 words per minute before pauses. Replace with **“For work, and for the work you make for yourself.”**
- 03, 0–4 seconds: 13 words in four seconds, roughly 195 words per minute. A shorter option is **“The photo kept the scene. Let's keep the feeling.”** For this particular mountain image, the gold/blues opening above is even more specific.

Those are script calculations, not a recorded performance assessment. A dry human read is still necessary. Do not make every route speak at the same pace: the founder can sound direct, the lesson patient, the joke spare and the editorial piece observational.

## Mobile legibility

At 360-pixel width the supplied 04 cover's main headline and question read well. The very small top descriptor, footer and poster detail are texture. They must not carry essential status, category or claim qualifications. “I'm building” in the main ending already does more useful work than the tiny development footer.

For upcoming native UI shots, do not scale a full desktop into a narrow vertical frame and expect the controls to remain readable. Establish the app once, then crop to one control plus the part of the image that changes. Show enough spatial context that the punch-in still feels like the same operation. Return to the clean image after the action.

Use a large enough comparison label to identify the same source and state. Do not depend on left/right alone: many viewers will see only one panel after a crop. For this subtle treatment, a full-frame wipe or sequential hold will communicate more than two half-width landscapes. Keep crop, exposure of the presentation itself and transition neutral; avoid a flashy wipe that exaggerates the difference.

Platform overlays still need an actual placement preview. An internal safe-area grid is a useful design aid, not proof that every device and placement will display the same clear area. The final check is whether the task, useful wording and CTA remain readable in the intended native surface.

## Production bottlenecks and the efficient route through them

1. **Canonical asset selection.** Create the actual review/export input list first, so the failed finish cannot accidentally re-enter. Record source, actual output, tool role and date. This is currently a higher risk than another visual revision.
2. **One verified project bank.** Retain the corrected photo, actual board deliveries and independently verified November 2 native Timeline excerpt. Decide whether 02 is the truthful three-form casebook or a newly captured fresh three-slide delivery route. Resolve that before cutting its hero; the casebook does not require inventing a fresh Timeline operation.
3. **Continuous actions.** A finished endpoint and a source-code control do not create footage of a gesture. P03, P04, P05 and P06 need explicit states: captured action, still only, or planned. Substituting animated UI would create a credibility problem.
4. **Founder session.** 01 needs face and voice; the optional 04 skit needs reaction takes; 05 can use hands or remain graphic. Group these into one short session with exact lines and a shot checklist. The user should see which campaign each take serves. A planning estimate is useful, but it is not recorded production time.
5. **Sound and pacing.** Audition the existing original 04 sound against actual playback; the metadata does not prove the joke lands. For narrated routes, record clean speech before fine-timing the graphics.
6. **Three types of board.** Keep creative-direction sheets, publishable carousel slides and editable native Looksi boards distinct in filenames and the tracker. Each is useful; they are different deliverables.
7. **Second review.** Judge the actual remaining renders, not only their scripts. Review both a small contact sheet for system coherence and individual phone-size frames for readability. Then inspect playback for timing, audio and held results.

## Agency-level completion standard for the next round

The user asked for several complete campaigns, including boards, images, videos and production tracking—not only a strategy. An adequate final package should make it effortless to identify the current image, current board and current video for each of the five styles; distinguish a publishable graphic piece from an animatic requiring filming; open the editable source; read the matched caption; and see exactly what remains to shoot or capture.

A truthful, specific preview is a valid production artifact when the matching dependencies are tracked. It should not be sold internally as a finished product demonstration. Conversely, the finished original graphic 04 should not remain mislabeled as waiting for founder footage merely because an earlier script expected a skit.

For 02 in particular, do not assert **all native, all finished, same fresh run** until that complete evidence exists. The verified photo endpoint, today's six-board deliveries and verified November 2 native motion excerpt are useful pieces with different scopes. A well-edited casebook can use all three. Preserve their value by describing each accurately, rather than implying one fresh continuous run.
