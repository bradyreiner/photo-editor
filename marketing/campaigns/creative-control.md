# Campaign: The creative future is yours

**Campaign promise:** Looksy’s local creative agent can help move an idea forward while the maker directs the brief, reviews the result, and chooses what happens next.

**Audience:** Independent photographers, designers, and visual creators curious about AI but protective of their taste and control.

**Campaign line:** “The creative future is yours.”

**Make it believable:** Every hero edit starts from a real source file and a real request. Capture the current app, the actual local model state, the generated or edited result from that run, the founder’s real correction, and the chosen final. The app remains the star, but let the founder talk in their own words. One imperfect first result can be more persuasive than a flawless fake demo.


**Production status:** These are shoot briefs. Initial review cuts use labeled source imagery and development previews. See [capture status](../evidence/current-capture-status.md) for the recorded evidence and remaining capture work.

## Capture brief

Create one small project from a rights-cleared photo you made yourself: for example, a still-life coffee setup, a plant in a window, a portrait with a signed release, or a night street scene. Use a supported source file, preferably a genuine camera RAW file for one version. Set a plain-language creative direction such as “make this feel like a quiet, warm editorial image; keep the mug shape and label accurate.” Do one real agent run. If the first draft misses, say exactly what is wrong and make one correction in the UI or through the agent. Keep the choice and final adjustment visible.

Record at 1440p or higher if practical, with the cursor visible and notifications hidden. Record a clean screen capture, a separate founder camera angle, a still of the source, the actual before/after, and the export. Do not invent model output, fabricate a “one-click” outcome, or imply every result is autonomous. If saying the run stayed on this computer, repeat the full capture with internet disconnected and preserve the evidence. Otherwise use the narrower wording in the scripts (“local model” / “local agent”).

**Rights and asset notes:** Founder-owned source image is preferred. For recognizable people, collect a release covering paid and organic social use. If using stock, record the specific file, current license, source page, download date, and attribution before editing; do not rely on a provider homepage as proof for a particular asset. Keep any licensed trend audio in a separate organic export; use an ad-cleared track or no music in paid versions.
