# Care Comes Back

A 64-second, wordless animated commercial for **STILL**, a fictional repair shop.

**Mara** is an older repairer with silver hair, round spectacles and a rust-red
apron. She is practical, gently funny, and takes her time looking at things.
**Pip** is a discarded palm-sized service robot, with an oversized cream head,
teal faceplate and one damaged hand. His eyes and posture carry his performance.

The visual language is an illustrated miniature: rounded silhouettes, paper
texture, restrained outlines, warm amber interiors and rain-blue exteriors.
There is no spoken dialogue. Give looks and small choices time to register.

| 13–18 | Pip's eyes open, briefly too bright. Mara leans back in surprise. He copies her head tilt. She tilts the other way. He follows. Her shoulders lift in a small, involuntary laugh. | The failed chime completes its second note. Piano motif begins. |
| 18–25 | Days later. Mara is sewing a torn canvas bag. Pip eagerly pulls the thread too hard. She pauses, smiles, opens his palm and gently lowers it: easy. He tries again. This time he holds the other end just right. | Thread, cloth, a small comic pluck, then a settled musical answer. |
| 25–35 | Three brief seasons of ordinary work: Pip steadies a crooked lamp; Mara adjusts it and he proudly copies her. They water a plant, a little too enthusiastically. At the bench, Mara sews while he holds the other end. The plant has grown. The repaired things are being used. | Music finds its rhythm. Lamp click, watering drops, familiar two-note chime. |
| 35–42 | Another evening. Mara tries to thread a needle. Her hand shakes. She tries a second time, then lowers it. No melodramatic collapse. Pip looks from the needle to her face. His eyes soften. The room becomes still. | Piano thins to a few isolated notes. We hear the clock and her breath. |
| 42–49 | Pip places his repaired, red-banded hand beneath hers. He looks up, waiting. She looks at him, then lets her hand rest in his. Together they pass the thread through the needle. The same gentle lowering gesture from the lesson returns, reversed. | A quiet mechanical servo, thread slipping through, the completed two-note motif. |
| 49–58 | Mara smiles, eyes a little wet. Pip gives the familiar head tilt. She returns it. Wider: both at the glowing bench. She sews; he holds the other end. We pull back through the window into the blue evening. Rain has stopped. Their warm little light stays on. | Piano opens into a warm final chord. Shop ambience, a last drop from the eaves. |
| 58–64 | Cream paper. A small red line draws itself into a neat repair stitch. **Care has a way of coming back.** Then the fictional shop mark: **STILL / Repairs for a world worth keeping.** | The two-note chime, now played softly on piano. Natural decay. |

The repair service is the advertised product. The character's difficulty is not
magically cured; the resolution is shared work and accepted care. Pip waits for
Mara's response before helping. The last shot leaves both characters active.

## Production contract

Deliver a finished film, original instrumental score/foley, stills/contact sheet,
and editable native vector scenes with hierarchical character rigs and animation
curves. Work in a new project; preserve all existing trailer and app work.
The film uses authored vector geometry and native Qt rendering, without generated
video or downloaded character assets. Its handmade shading is an art direction,
not a claim of physical simulation. The full native app lifecycle and UI controls
must be distinguished from standalone renderer and source integration evidence.
