# The Perfect Picnic

A playful, wordless 54-second commercial for **KIN**, a fictional companion-robot
brand. **Nell**, a spirited girl in a mustard coat and red scarf, and **Pip**, a
very conscientious little robot, are going on a picnic.

**The want:** Pip wants to give Nell a perfect afternoon. His mistake is believing
that means everything has to go according to plan. Nell shows him another way.

| Time | Action and acting | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00–06 | A lovely park. Pip solemnly works through a tiny picnic checklist, checking each item against the blanket and cupcake. Nell swings a foot and watches his very serious little ritual with an affectionate grin. | Light piano and plucked strings; servo ticks. |
| 06–11 | Nell offers Pip a strawberry. He looks down at it, then back at her. A pause. She remembers he has no mouth. They share a look; she eats it with a grin. | A comic pluck, a two-note robot chirp, a playful musical answer. |
| 11–16 | One drop lands on Pip's head. He looks up. Another on the cupcake. Nell holds out her palm. Rain starts. Pip snaps into action; Nell looks delighted by the weather. | A drop, another drop, soft rain. The score becomes mischievous. |
| 16–23 | Pip deploys a tiny umbrella. Too small. He extends it; now absurdly large. It catches a gust and drags him sideways before escaping into the sky. Nell ducks, then watches it sail away. Pip slowly lowers his empty hand. | Ratchet, spring, fabric pop, rising wind, an airy whoosh. |
| 23–28 | Pip looks at the soggy blanket, then at Nell. His eyes droop. Nell notices a puddle. She puts one boot in it: a small splash. He looks over. She holds out her hand, inviting him. | Music briefly leaves space. One clear little splash. |
| 28–35 | Nell jumps. A joyful splash. Pip recoils, then peers at the ripples. She jumps again, laughing. A drop trickles down his faceplate. He looks at her, then at the puddle. His antenna rises. A decision. | Playful piano returns. Splash, patter, a hopeful two-note chirp. |
| 35–42 | Pip crouches with comic seriousness. Little pre-jump adjustments. Nell watches. He launches and lands in a gloriously oversized splash. Both disappear behind it. The water falls: Nell is stunned. A beat. Then her shoulders shake with laughter, and Pip does a delighted little bounce. | Springy servo wind-up, a big satisfying splash, silence for the reaction, laughter. |
| 42–48 | Wide. Both happily splash together. In the foreground, the cupcake is dry beneath Pip's tiny emergency umbrella. He pauses to straighten it, then runs back to Nell. The perfectionist is still himself; now he knows what matters. | The musical motif resolves warmly, rain and small splashes. |
| 48–54 | Warm paper. The tiny umbrella pops open above the mark. **KIN.** Then: **For the wonderfully unplanned.** | A last two-note chirp, played back by piano. |

The emotional payoff is Pip choosing shared fun over rescuing his plan. The last
cupcake gag pays off both his opening fussiness and the earlier failed umbrella.
There is no aging, loss, or medical difficulty. The humor comes from affectionate
character differences, not humiliation or an incompetent child.

## Visual and delivery direction

Original illustrated vector characters, articulated parented rigs, eye/head acting,
two-bone hand targets, squash/stretch, anticipation, airborne arcs, settling,
layered water animation, rain, camera reframing and paper shading. Native Qt scene
rendering; no generated-video shots. Original instrumental score and foley.
Deliver a complete MP4, editable shot/rig data and authoring source, contact sheet,
and focused renderer checks. Native app integration and standalone rendering have
separate qualification boundaries.
