# Nepal trailer asset shortlist

Use the yellow-jacket mountain portrait as the hero, the clean alpenglow peak for RAW/color work, and boats, flowers, flags, and the bridge to build a varied travel set. Six primary photographs and eight original video passages are selected, with three photo and two video alternates. The new Downloads set adds four preferred 4K passages and two 4K alternates.

[Open the local review gallery](../../renders/nepal-review/review.html) · [Recommended photo sheet](../../renders/nepal-review/recommended-photos.jpg) · [Source manifest](../../renders/nepal-review/shortlist.json)

## Review scope

Direct root media only; nested backup/card folders were not scanned.

All 559 camera previews and three temporal samples from every clip were visually inspected. Sixteen selected stills received larger previews; ten candidate clip passages received twelve temporal samples, with three ranges refined. This is a visual asset shortlist, not completed RAW development, continuous-motion/audio approval, publication, or a 4K source demonstration.

Photo previews use the camera-rendered image embedded in each CR2 with orientation applied; no grading or generative changes. Video review proxies are silent, 1280 pixels wide, with source timing retained. Originals remain on the SSD.

All 559 root RAW files are Canon EOS 80D 24 MP images. All 98 root MP4 files are 1920×1080 at 60000/1001 fps, approximately 59.94 fps. The SSD clips are 1080p; the additional Downloads clips below provide 3840×2160 sources. The 67 JPEG files have corresponding CR2 files and were not counted as separate photographs.

## Suggested use

- Main photo edit and text-mask composition: IMG_5106.CR2. Use IMG_5125.CR2 if the alternate portrait fits the final artwork better.
- Landscape RAW/color demonstration: IMG_5071.CR2; IMG_5053.CR2 is the layered-depth alternative.
- Carousel source set: hero portrait, clean peak, bridge, flags, boats, and flowers. Choose a smaller subset once the layout is designed.
- Primary 4K timeline source set: the Downloads trekking, flag-lined street, market, and night-square clips. Supplement with the SSD sunrise peak, bridge turn, flowers, boats, and valley where they serve the final film.
- Keep the fire and backlit-field material as accents rather than making every candidate appear.

Source identities and SHA-256 hashes are stored in the manifest. No source media was moved or edited, and no publication step was performed.
