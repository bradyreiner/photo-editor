## What changed after the integration feedback

- The main optical material is geometry, not a loader-video overlay. The native
  caustic centerline drives image aperture boundaries and panel reveals. Those
  shapes actually crop/reveal source media and move with the media frame.
- The primary photo persists across the sequence. It becomes a mask/pixel edit,
  an editable graphic, a program frame, a carousel cover, a delivery, a search
  result and a revisable agent result. Pose and camera changes are continuous.
- Actual RGB channel isolation/registration is used for the color-engine
  visual; the source/mask/type planes separate during composition. This is a
  graphic explanation, not an engine accuracy demonstration.
- Poster typography and design carry into video titles and the carousel. The
  timeline has moving picture, animated title/effect cues, trims, keyframes and
  independent audio tracks. One designed carousel board contains moving video.
- The five-board carousel has linked accent changes, board navigation, layouts
  and export context. It is a separate substantial authoring chapter.
- Search occurs after the completed manual delivery. It shows content query,
  selected collection, local model context, match explanation, saved-description
  reuse/detail checking and an explicit selected-edit handoff.
- The optional agent has a selected task, sample/direction review, approved
  batch, native controls/layers/history and a manual revision. No generated
  replacement photo/video is depicted.
- The standalone player includes the whole film, music, seeking and chapter
  navigation. Older 72s/12s/14s outputs remain reference studies.

## Review scope

The film is fully assembled, but its aesthetic quality remains subject to user
review. The controls include native-source browser adapters; workspace states,
RAW response, mask, agent/search results, progress and output settings are
illustrative animation. Inspect corresponding native operations and outputs
before using the film as public capability proof. No universal format support,
realtime 4K qualification, camera-RAW export or industry certification is implied
by this assembly receipt.

The produced stock music selection is still a review cue. Its full edit and
crossfade are recorded in `../../animated-trailer/music-source.json`.

## Encoded verification

The final MP4 was decoded through all 7,200 video frames and 120 seconds of
stereo audio without a decoder error. Metadata confirms 1080×1920, H.264,
60 fps, and AAC stereo at 48 kHz. Soundtrack mastering and source/output hashes
are recorded in `out/complete-film-verification.json` in the rendering project.
The native browser player reports a 0–120-second seekable range, active unmuted
playback with no media error, and successful jumps to the carousel and local
agent chapters. These checks verify the encoded artifact and its player, not
the native application's advertised editing behavior.
