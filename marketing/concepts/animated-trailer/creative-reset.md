## What the film must establish

Looksy is a purpose-built photo, design, and video editor built around a custom
color engine. A photographer can finish a RAW shoot; a designer can build an
editable poster or web graphic from the same material; an editor can construct
and finish a layered video production. These are complete creative disciplines
inside the same app. Library volume and batch workflow connect them. Optional
local assistance arrives after the manual capabilities have earned attention.

The current film under-explains the product. Generic copy such as “Every
adjustment. Your call.” does not establish RAW processing, the custom color
engine, source-resolution work, color-managed delivery, animation, or effects.
A timeline illustration with an Effects label does not demonstrate effects.
The illustrated paper workstation also obscures the actual dark optical brand.

Use the current native Theme.qml and supplied brand boards, rather than the
rejected Spectrum redesign exploration or the paper style study:

- Deep black `#050505`, graphite `#181818`, raised graphite `#242424`, white
  `#F3F3F3`. Brand RGB: red `#FF3B30`, green `#30D158`, blue `#3282FF`.
- Dark working surfaces, precise compact rectangular controls, clear values,
  editable stacks, and the existing Looksy wordmark with its additive RGB mark.
- Optical light is a central motion material: flowing ridges, additive channel
  overlaps, chromatic fringes, and a bright white core where light combines.
  Compose it with layered fields/shaders, not thin multicolor decoration.
- Keep reference photographs and the area around a grading decision neutral.
  Light can occupy the wider brand world, travel through a transition, or mark
  a committed edit; it must not tint the apparent edited result.
- Retain abstract 2D/vector illustration, smooth elastic choreography, depth
  planes, camera translation, parallax, and occasional perspective rotation.
  No character, photorealistic 3D set, voiceover, or mascot-led introduction.
- Rebuild illustrated app surfaces from the actual workspace hierarchy,
  controls, icons, typography proportions, and panel geometry. This remains the
  fully animated trailer; the separate native recording proves real operation.

The intended feeling is a dark optical creative instrument that becomes playful
in motion. Text, images, masks, pixels, and timeline geometry should transform
into the next scene. Photographs are source material being worked on, not
standalone cards floating past explanatory headlines.

## How the animation gets built

Light follows the pictured work and hands off into the next scene. The complete assembly uses the native optical wave equations as actual media
apertures and panel geometry, rather than placing the loading field over the
film. A persistent source frame connects photo, mask, design, production,
carousel and assistance states. The earlier **Light-to-Library** study remains
a separate experiment. [App-element choreography](app-elements.md#light-as-scene-choreography)
maps the remaining loader, completion, camera and object handoffs.

Remotion remains the deterministic scene/timing/render host. It is not the
visual technique. Author a continuous scene graph with SVG paths and masks,
vector UI geometry, actual moving media, shared image coordinates, and a common
camera transform. Use Canvas/WebGL light fields for refracted ridges, layered
RGB emission, and optical transitions. These effects should be repeatable at
render time and composite correctly; a random animated gradient is insufficient.

Every handoff needs a storyboard drawing of the outgoing frame, the midpoint,
and the landing. Carry the same object through it. Build shape changes,
clipping reveals, perspective, follow-through, and purposeful holds into the
scene. Crossfades and translating screenshots do not satisfy the continuity.

Production sequence used for the complete assembly:

1. Complete the capability coverage map before reducing the story again. Draw
   six dark key frames: RAW/color engine, multi-image composition/layers,
   timeline/effects, carousel authoring, AI-search results, and native agent
   revision. Show actual controls, source media, and deliberate finished work.
2. Make one 6–8 second high-quality motion proof linking RAW → pixels → layered
   design with the optical light system. This must prove the replacement motion
   language, not serve as another misleadingly short trailer deliverable.
3. Produce a complete rough animatic with the complete proposed 120-second sequence, temporary text and
   real media, and the produced electronic review music. Check information
   hierarchy, breathing room, and phrase changes before polishing every shot.
4. Build full-resolution scenes, add selective tactile sound, refine music
   cuts, and render a complete watchable film. Keep full-film playback available
   throughout so later sections are never hidden behind a short experiment.

Acceptance: immediately identifiable dark Looksy identity; at least one concrete
image/result change for each major claim; no decorative caustic over a grading
comparison; no static-photo-only timeline; no feature “demonstrated” only by a
label; no detached slide/copy reset at every scene boundary; editor before agent.

## Claim matching for production

Current source inspection supports using the custom-engine, RAW, pixel/layer,
photo-effect, carousel-authoring, visual-search, and timeline subjects. It does not establish that the entire
advertised production is qualified. Pair final copy with the real app exercise
and inspected result. Keep technical evidence outside the film itself.

- RAW editing plus native/source-resolution output does not mean exporting a
  newly encoded camera RAW file. Use “RAW editing” and “full-resolution export.”
- Use the exact source resolution from metadata; do not call every photo 24 MP.
- “4K video” needs a decoded 3840×2160 (or explicitly chosen 4096×2160) output
  of the depicted production. It does not promise realtime 4K performance.
- Match sRGB, Rec.709, and the specific Sony input profiles to the pictured
  operations. Do not imply every log format, HDR display qualification, or
  perfect preview/export parity from labels alone. Current timeline notes
  explicitly retain combined-workflow and parity qualifications.
- Depict effects supported in the selected photo/video path. Do not transfer
  a photo effect into the video inspector merely because it exists elsewhere.
- Verify pixel-paint/erase, source coordinates, mask refinement, layer order,
  export, undo, save/reopen, and actual timeline combinations in the app.
- Optional local inference is the pictured mode, not an absolute promise that
  every feature/configuration never networks. The depicted agent edits existing
  source media; no generative imagery or video is part of the demonstration.
