# Media rights

The maintained [media rights guide](media/rights-guide.md) explains the source pack and intake process. The machine-readable [source ledger](media/sources.json) records asset-specific license evidence, hashes, and use status.

The original generated landscape, supplied brand artwork, and sample-free soundbed have separate provenance records under `media/generated/`, `media/brand-provenance.json`, and `media/audio/`. A copyright license claim is recorded separately from final ad-use clearance.
