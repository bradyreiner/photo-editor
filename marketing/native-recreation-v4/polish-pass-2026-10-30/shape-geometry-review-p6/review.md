## Guarded geometry proposal

[`duplicate-shapes-p6-proposal.commands-fragment.json`](duplicate-shapes-p6-proposal.commands-fragment.json) contains 25 targets in five small scene groups: 196s (9 clips), 218s (9), 226s (3), 232s (1), and 244s (3). Each group is a separate batch under the 16-action limit. The proposal covers only the visible groups with a definite reference match; it leaves 12 of the 37 candidates untouched: G42 at 199.5s; all seven 208s candidates; three fine rules at 232s; and the transparent G9 at 244s.

For each selected clip, the proposed change is restricted to a neutral full-canvas outer frame (`width/height=1`, offsets/rotation/perspective/corner radius `0`, scale `1`). It leaves the inner title-shape geometry, title position, fill/stroke, clip timing, opacity, mask, effects, color, source, and text untouched. The target shape’s expected size and center remain those already stored in `titleShapeWidth/Height` and `titlePositionX/Y`.

For animated candidates, the fragment uses `setTimelineClipAnimation` with empty keyframes to clear only frame-offset/opacity channels that `setTimelineClipFrame` could otherwise key at the current playhead. It then sets the neutral frame and restores the exact P6 samples. Animated frameOffsetX/Y values are rebased by subtracting the old base frame offset; opacity samples are unchanged. The selected P6 channels are linear per-frame samples and use no custom Bezier/roving handles. The active output variant must remain `landscape`; both main and landscape frame state must pass the exact guard.
