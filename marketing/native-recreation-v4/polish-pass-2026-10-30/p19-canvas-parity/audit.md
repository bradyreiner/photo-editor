### Group-transform gap

`CanvasPolish.tsx` applies a coherent group transform to the CampaignPoster: `perspective(1800px)`, `rotateY(...)`, `rotateZ(-3deg)`, `scale(.6)`, all about `transformOrigin: "0 0"`. P16 child clips are separate top-level native clips with empty group IDs/group-parent IDs and zero track-group transforms. A per-clip rotation or offset would use different pivots and would not reproduce the poster group’s geometry. Do not scatter individual frame transforms across the poster children. A native grouped transform with a shared pivot/perspective is the correct future capability.
