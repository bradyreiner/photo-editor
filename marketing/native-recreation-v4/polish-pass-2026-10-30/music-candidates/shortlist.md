# Soundtrack shortlist for the 298-second Looksy trailer

Research date: 2026-10-30. These are metadata-based candidates, not listened or approved picks. “Sun in Your Eyes” remains a style reference; the existing `EDMDetectionMode` stays as an A/B control and is not a recommendation.

**Download follow-up:** Public audio candidates are now downloaded with ffprobe/SHA-256 manifests and bounded audition excerpts in [downloaded-candidates.md](downloaded-candidates.md). That is the actionable shortlist; this file preserves the earlier ENDE.APP metadata leads, which still require a creator account for downloads.

## 1. Tranceverse — Sascha Ende (best fit to audition first)

- **Length:** 4:57 (297 seconds), one second shorter than the 298-second cut.
- **Creator-page description:** instrumental, fast, old-school trance/techno with modern sounds; pulsating synth arpeggios, four-on-the-floor beat, atmospheric pads, and a progressive build. The creator marks structural events at 0:48, 1:52, and 3:44, which could support a single cue across the full story rather than arbitrary track changes.
- **Fit:** closest match found to the requested sustained, uplifting electronic drive, and the duration naturally fits an intentional one-second opening sonic mark followed by the full cue. Do not speed it up, loop it, or add a second bed.
- **License/credit:** ENDE.APP track page says free for commercial use under CC BY 4.0 and specifies attribution. Suggested credit: “Tranceverse by Sascha Ende — ende.app — CC BY 4.0.” The creator’s current FAQ says attribution is voluntary, but use the track-page credit to be conservative.
- **Source:** [official track page](https://ende.app/en/song/5223-tranceverse); [creator license](https://ende.app/en/standard-license); [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## 2. Tivinize — Sascha Ende (near-exact, gentler alternate)

- **Length:** 4:59 (299 seconds), one second longer than the cut.
- **Creator-page description:** instrumental, moderate electronic; evolving synth pads and a modern beat build from an atmospheric opening into a confident, hopeful theme. The page describes an outro that echoes the intro and lets the synth texture fade.
- **Fit:** almost exact runtime and cohesive arc, but the creator labels it moderate rather than fast. It may suit a more polished, spacious cut; it is a weaker match if the film needs the persistent punch of “Sun in Your Eyes.” Only trim one second after listening confirms the natural tail allows it.
- **License/credit:** free commercial use under CC BY 4.0; the track page asks for attribution. Suggested credit: “Tivinize by Sascha Ende — ende.app — CC BY 4.0.”
- **Source:** [official track page](https://ende.app/en/song/62-tivinize); [creator license](https://ende.app/en/standard-license); [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## 3. Let Me Be Free (instrumental) — Sascha Ende (backup, needs a larger natural trim)

- **Length:** 5:10 (310 seconds), twelve seconds longer than the cut.
- **Creator-page description:** fast instrumental electronic with pulsing synths, steady four-on-the-floor beat, atmospheric pads, and a dynamic build. Published cue points include a breakdown at 1:05 and a peak around 4:15.
- **Fit:** credible energetic alternate if auditioning shows a clean ending twelve seconds earlier; otherwise exclude it. It is less convenient than the first two and should not be forced to fit with time-stretching or looping.
- **License/credit:** free commercial use under CC BY 4.0; the track page asks for attribution. Suggested credit: “Let Me Be Free (instrumental) by Sascha Ende — ende.app — CC BY 4.0.”
- **Source:** [official track page](https://ende.app/en/song/165-let-me-be-free-instrumental); [creator license](https://ende.app/en/standard-license); [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
