Treat the screenshots as evidence of what the exposed app displayed during those seeks, not proof that the corresponding frame was freshly decoded or that an export would contain it. That file has an AAC stream, but decoded native audio samples for the 10-second prefix had RMS 0.0 while the reference measured −20.28 dBFS. This is signal analysis only; no listening claim is made.

## Overall judgment

The reference has a distinctive art direction worth preserving: dark editorial space, the moving rainbow light field, restrained lime microtype, condensed display headlines, serif travel captions, and concrete photography/design examples. The trailer communicates a broad creative workflow and the opening 10-second native prefix is visually close to its reference.

The main risk is production readiness, not concept. Current native evidence shows unresolved or unqualified media at multiple seeks, copy collisions, a blank-looking popup, a stale displayed frame, and an incomplete endcard. Because presentation identity is unverified and there is no full native export, those observations identify review targets rather than prove every defect is baked into a final file. The verified-overlap defect around 45.7 seconds was independently traced to stacked title clips; root has since applied a source correction and captured [`native-raw-layout-v1/t00045.733.png`](native-raw-layout-v1/t00045.733.png). That corrected frame now clears the left-copy overlap, but the heading remains too wide and crowds the photo edge.

At 4:58, the reference is long for a launch trailer. The full-frame sample sequence has attractive variety, but too many consecutive seconds hold on a static edit/panel or a single state. Tighten repeated holds, and make each feature shot show an operation and its result. Preserve the existing movie and approved visual language; sharpen the narrative around one creator taking work from camera originals through a finished, adaptable deliverable.

## Story and pacing

The reference order is coherent at the feature level: brand/boat and photo editing; library/culling; Canvas compositing and poster design; adaptable campaign outputs; travel video and color/effects; Gaussian camera motion; optional agent assistance; brand close. Retain those approved chapters and visual motifs.

The trailer would feel more like one finished story if the cut follows one body of work: capture, choose keepers, refine a subject, turn a selection into campaign layouts, continue into a travel edit, then review/hand off. Current chapter cards sometimes announce capability before the moving image proves the result. Reduce repeated static holds and let a handful of deliberate before/action/after beats carry the message. Use the portrait reference’s intentional title relocation and image reflow as the guide; do not center-crop the landscape composition.

## Prioritized polish list

1. **P0 — Qualify the actual full native movie.** Produce current 298-second landscape and portrait exports from the same saved native revision. Confirm decoded duration/frame count, source readiness and decoded frame identity at all chapter boundaries; inspect adjacent frames through transitions and compare both orientations. Current exports cover only the 10-second opening, so the full film and portrait delivery remain unqualified.
2. **P0 — Resolve preview/source readiness defects.** Re-seek 00:05, 01:08, 02:25, 02:53, 03:42 and 04:49 after media readiness is true. Confirm current source identity and displayed Program frame before editing. Keep screenshot evidence with requested and actual seek/timecode recorded.
3. **P1 — Repair native typography layouts.** Use explicit line breaks for 00:30 and 04:25; give the 01:24 headline top breathing room. Apply the prepared title-only fragment only after live revision/hash and clip baseline checks; it is intentionally unapplied.
4. **P1 — Finish the feature proof.** Ensure library/culling, exposure, inpaint, poster editing and color/effects show a visible action/result, with readable UI at 1080p and no blank popup/placeholder states. Preserve attractive hero images and uncluttered beauty frames.
5. **P1 — Restore the closing identity.** Show the full Looksy mark and CTA for a deliberate hold, then end cleanly. Verify the last picture and audio tails in both orientations.
6. **P1 — Complete and mix the soundtrack.** The reference has stereo AAC; the native 10-second prefix AAC decodes to zero RMS. Inspect the full native mix for music/ambience/SFX, intended synchronization, joins and tail. Audio was not auditioned in this review.
7. **P2 — Tighten the cut toward a launch length.** Remove duplicate holds and demonstrations that repeat the same state; preserve the 4:58 full cut only if the intended format is an extended product tour. Build a short trailer from the strongest capture → edit → deliver arc, with moving footage and outcome proof between interface chapters.
