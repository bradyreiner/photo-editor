# CC0 1.0 official deed snapshot

- Reviewed: 2026-10-07
- Official deed: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Legal code: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode>

The deed says the copyright holder dedicates the work to the public domain by waiving copyright and related rights to the extent allowed by law. It says the work may be copied, modified, distributed, and performed, including for commercial purposes, without asking permission.

The same deed says CC0 does not affect patent or trademark rights, or other people's rights such as publicity and privacy rights. It says the work is provided without warranties, and users should not imply endorsement by the author or affirmer. Creative Commons also says it does not verify the copyright status of individual works.

This is why the manifest records the asset-page license claim separately from release and endorsement review. It is not a blanket confirmation that every pictured person, building, product design, mark, or location is cleared for a paid ad.
