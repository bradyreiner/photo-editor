Policy

Privacy

Local editing and purchase privacy

Looksy is designed to edit photographs locally. The application does not require a Looksy account and does not automatically upload photographs, catalogs, edits, or diagnostic logs.

Information stored on your device

The application stores preferences, catalogs, edit recipes, previews, caches, managed PostgreSQL data, migration backups, session-recovery state, and local diagnostic logs in its application data locations. You control those files through your operating system and the Storage page in Preferences.

Diagnostics and support bundles

Release builds retain a limited number of local text-log sessions. A support bundle is created only when you choose that action. It contains system and application metadata plus recent text logs; it does not include photo bytes. Logs may contain local file paths, so review the folder before sharing it.

Website and downloads

The hosting provider may process normal web-server information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamps for security and delivery. The download page queries the public GitHub Releases API to locate signed installers. GitHub and the hosting provider process those requests under their own policies.

Publisher

Looksy is published by Publisher identity pending release approval (the “Publisher”). Contact the Publisher through the support page.

Payments

If you choose to support development or buy a license, checkout is hosted by Lemon Squeezy. It handles checkout, receipts, and order recovery under its own privacy policy. The Publisher does not receive or store full payment-card numbers. The provider may send the Publisher transaction identifiers, contact details, amounts, tax information, and refund status needed to administer the purchase.

Support requests

If you contact support, the Publisher receives the information you send. Do not include original photos or catalogs unless support specifically requests them and you agree. Support material is retained only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may evaluate the application without purchasing, avoid sending a support bundle, delete local logs and caches, and request deletion of support or purchase contact information where applicable law provides that right. Payment and tax records may need to be retained for legally required periods.

Contact

Use the contact shown on the support page. This policy should be reviewed by the release owner and qualified counsel before the first commercial sale.
