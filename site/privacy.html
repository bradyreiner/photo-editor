Policy

Privacy

Local editing and purchase privacy

Looksy edits photographs locally. Guest evaluation uses an anonymous installation credential; managed paid access validates a purchase license key. These access requests do not upload photographs, catalogs, or edit recipes. Email/password sign-in is not required. Optional crash reporting is described separately below.

Information stored on your device

The application stores preferences, catalogs, edit recipes, previews, caches, managed PostgreSQL data, migration backups, session-recovery state, and local diagnostic logs in its application data locations. You control those files through your operating system and the Storage page in Preferences.

Diagnostics and support bundles

Release builds retain a limited number of local text-log sessions. A support bundle is created only when you choose that action. It contains system and application metadata plus recent text logs; it does not include photo bytes. Logs may contain local file paths, so review the folder before sharing it.

License service

Managed access sends an installation credential and, during activation or recovery, the license key to the Publisher's license service hosted on Cloudflare Workers and D1. The service stores a hash of the installation credential, purchase/customer identifiers, the purchase email supplied by Lemon Squeezy, entitlement and subscription state, installation platform/app version/last-seen time, and staff support audit records. Raw license keys are transient validation inputs and are not stored in the operations database. Installation credentials stay in the operating system's secure credential store. Access uses a random installation identity rather than hardware serial numbers.

Optional crash reports

Where the release offers automatic crash reporting, it is off by default and requires your consent. Reports may be processed by Sentry and include release/channel, an opaque verified customer identifier, and fixed workflow events. Photo files, catalogs, edit recipes, and text logs are not intentionally attached. Native crash dumps can contain process memory and personal information. Disabling reporting removes the dedicated queued reports where possible; the application reports a cleanup failure. Detailed support bundles remain a separate, user-created action.

Website and downloads

The hosting provider may process normal web-server information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamps for security and delivery. The download page queries the public GitHub Releases API to locate signed installers. GitHub and the hosting provider process those requests under their own policies.

Publisher

Looksy is published by Publisher identity pending release approval (the “Publisher”). Contact the Publisher through the support page.

Payments

If you choose to support development or buy a license, checkout is hosted by Lemon Squeezy. It handles checkout, receipts, and order recovery under its own privacy policy. The Publisher does not receive or store full payment-card numbers. The provider may send the Publisher transaction identifiers, contact details, amounts, tax information, and refund status needed to administer the purchase.

Support requests

If you contact support, the Publisher receives the information you send. Do not include original photos or catalogs unless support specifically requests them and you agree. Support material is retained only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.

Your choices and retention

You may evaluate without purchasing, disable optional crash reporting, avoid sending a support bundle, and delete local logs and caches. Contact support to request access to or removal of hosted purchase/contact information after ownership is verified. Operational deletion removes or anonymizes eligible personal fields while retaining restricted transaction, revocation, and audit records needed to administer access and prevent deleted access from being restored by a later billing event. Payment-provider and diagnostic-processor requests may require separate handling. Successful webhook receipts are normally retained for 90 days; transient rate-limit buckets expire after two minutes. Customer/license/device and staff audit records otherwise remain until a reviewed retention/deletion process applies. Payment and tax records may need to be retained for required periods.

Contact

Use the contact shown on the support page. This policy should be reviewed by the release owner and qualified counsel before the first commercial sale.
