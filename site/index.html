Alpha scope

A complete photo workflow is the product.

Import and cull

Bring in focused libraries, review photographs quickly, and keep catalog state local.

Tone and color

Work with exposure, tonal controls, curves, profiles, and non-destructive recipes.

Masks and layers

Refine edits through selection-aware masks, local adjustments, and a visible active target.

Export and recover

Queue exports, preserve edits across relaunches, and create backups before catalog migrations.
