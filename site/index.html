01 / THIS IS LOOKSY NOW

ONE STUDIO.
MANY WAYS TO MAKE.

Move between photographic editing, graphic design, motion, and spatial work. Keep the underlying pieces editable.

01 / PHOTO & RETOUCH

Develop the photograph.
Refine the details.

Work with RAW, tone curves, white balance, color grading, and image detail. Use selections, masks, and retouching for changes that belong in one part of the frame.

  • Global and local adjustments
  • Selection tools and layer masks
  • Healing, cloning, and inpainting tools
  • Presets, LUTs, and reference-based shoot matching
02 / LAYERED DESIGN

A canvas for more
than photo adjustments.

Combine photographs, editable typography, shapes, vector paths, and paint. Build a poster, a composite, a thumbnail, or a layout without flattening every decision into one image.

  • Image, text, shape, and adjustment layers
  • Typography and vector masks
  • Blending, effects, and layer styles
  • Reusable image components
03 / BOARDS & CAMPAIGNS

Design the whole set.
Keep the pieces connected.

Work across several boards in one document. Build a carousel, a visual story, or a coordinated set of graphics with shared imagery and a consistent design system.

  • Multi-board and shared-canvas layouts
  • Guides, alignment, and reusable content
  • Brand-kit and template tools
  • Multiple output sizes and proof PDFs
04 / VIDEO & AUDIO

A timeline you
can really edit.

Arrange video, photographs, titles, captions, and audio on tracks. Shape the timing with editorial tools, animate properties with keyframes, and finish with effects and color.

  • Trimming, ripple, roll, slip, and slide
  • Keyframes and Bézier curve editing
  • Caption tools and audio mixing
  • Color scopes and delivery preflight
05 / SCENE & 3D MOTION

Go beyond
the flat frame.

Open Gaussian scenes and work with virtual cameras, saved views, lighting, and lens character. Use 3D Motion to build depth-based camera movement from a photograph.

  • Gaussian PLY import and capture workflows
  • Camera paths and saved shots
  • Focus, lens, color, and film controls
  • Photo, film, and frame-sequence output
06 / LOCAL AGENT STUDIO

Put a brief
beside the work.

Choose the target, describe what you want to make, and add a visual reference. Agent Studio brings a conversational workflow into the editor, with local-model options and reviewable drafts.

  • Target and reference selection
  • Natural-language creative requests
  • Local-model setup and status
  • Draft review and scoped tool access

Agent and model-based tools need a compatible runtime or model. Available actions depend on the build and configuration.

BUILT AROUND THE THINGS YOU MAKE

THE PHOTO IS
JUST THE BEGINNING.

Start with an image, a design brief, a shoot, or a sequence. Choose the workspace that suits the next step.

A PHOTO STORY

Organize → edit → publish

Cull the shoot, refine your selects, lay them out across boards, then export the series in the sizes you need.

A DESIGN PROJECT

Compose → reuse → deliver

Combine type, images, and graphics. Keep layers editable, reuse components, and produce a coordinated set of outputs.

A MOTION PROJECT

Sequence → animate → finish

Cut clips and audio in Timeline, animate design elements, or explore a virtual camera in Scene before making the final output.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your catalog, media, and creative documents stay on your computer. Model downloads and optional integrations have their own setup; ordinary editing doesn't require uploading your library.
