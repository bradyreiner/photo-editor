LOOKSY / NATIVE DESKTOP APP

EDIT PHOTOS.
DESIGN GRAPHICS.
CUT VIDEO.

Looksy brings photo editing, layered graphic design, video, and 3D motion into one desktop app.

For photographers and visual creators who want to organize their media, make the work, and export it from the same place. Your library stays on your computer.

See what you can make ↓Public alpha

01 / PHOTO EDITING & RETOUCHING

CONTROL COLOR.
EDIT WITH MASKS.
RETOUCH DETAILS.

Develop RAW files, balance exposure and white balance, and shape the look with curves and color grading. Use selections and masks to edit a face, a sky, or a background separately.

Correct the photograph
Adjust light, color, detail, and lens corrections.
Work on one area
Use local adjustments, brushes, and layer masks.
Retouch and match a set
Heal, clone, or use inpainting; copy edits or compare reference-based matching proposals.
Next: build a design from your images →

02 / GRAPHIC DESIGN & BOARDS

DESIGN POSTERS,
CAROUSELS &
CAMPAIGN GRAPHICS.

Combine photographs, editable type, shapes, paths, and paint on a layered canvas. Arrange several boards in one document to design a carousel, a menu, or a coordinated set of graphics.

Keep the design editable
Move, restyle, mask, and blend individual layers.
Build a consistent set
Use reusable components, templates, brand kits, and shared-canvas layouts.
Prepare the deliverables
Export multiple sizes and create a proof PDF for review.
Next: edit video and audio →

03 / VIDEO EDITING & AUDIO

CUT VIDEO.
ANIMATE TITLES.
MIX YOUR AUDIO.

Build a film or social video from clips, photographs, titles, captions, and sound. Arrange tracks, trim the timing, animate properties with keyframes, and finish the sequence with effects and color.

Edit the sequence
Use blade, ripple, roll, slip, and slide tools.
Shape movement and sound
Edit Bézier animation curves, mix audio, and work with captions.
Check before delivery
Inspect color scopes and run export preflight.
Next: animate photos and 3D captures →

04 / SCENE & 3D MOTION

ANIMATE PHOTOS.
EXPLORE CAPTURES
WITH A 3D CAMERA.

Use 3D Motion to create depth-based camera movement from a photograph. In Scene, open a Gaussian capture, choose virtual camera positions, and connect saved views into a camera path.

Start with a source
A photograph for depth motion, or a Gaussian PLY for Scene.
Choose the camera and look
Set framing, focus, lens character, lighting, color, and film controls.
Render the result
Export a still image, film, or numbered frame sequence.
Next: work with a local AI agent →

05 / OPTIONAL LOCAL AGENT STUDIO

DESCRIBE THE EDIT.
ADD A REFERENCE.
REVIEW THE DRAFT.

Agent Studio gives you a conversational way to work in Looksy. Choose the photo or project, explain what you want to make, and add a reference to guide the direction.

Define the target
Choose the work the agent should act on.
Give creative direction
Write a brief and optionally supply a reference photo or look.
Stay involved
Review proposed work and drafts before accepting changes.

Requires a compatible model and runtime. Available agent actions depend on the build and configuration.
