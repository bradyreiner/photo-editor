Refund policy

Purchase and support refunds

Evaluation downloads are available without payment. A paid license is required for personal or commercial production use as described in the license terms.

Publisher

Refund obligations are administered by Publisher identity pending release approval (the “Publisher”). Use the support link below to contact the Publisher.

Optional support payments

If you made an optional support payment by mistake, were charged twice, or simply changed your mind, contact support within 30 days with the payment-provider receipt. The Publisher will request a refund through the payment provider unless fraud, a prior chargeback, or another legal restriction prevents it.

Purchased licenses

The checkout page identifies the license and price before payment. Unless a longer mandatory period applies, contact support within 14 days to request a refund. A fully refunded purchase ends the associated paid license. Your photographs and exports remain yours; refund processing does not delete local files.

Subscriptions

Use the configured billing portal on the support page to cancel future renewal or update payment details. Cancellation and a refund are separate actions. Canceled access continues through the provider's verified paid-through date unless refunded or disabled. A confirmed refund removes new editing access on verification; viewing, saving, and exporting existing work remain available.

Consumer rights

This policy does not limit statutory cancellation, refund, warranty, or other consumer rights. Payment-provider processing time and currency conversion can affect when funds appear.

Request a refund

Use the support page and include the transaction or receipt identifier. Never send a full payment-card number.
