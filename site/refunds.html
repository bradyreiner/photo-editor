Refund policy

Effective July 10, 2026 · Public alpha

The public alpha is available without payment. Financial support is optional.

Optional support payments

If you made an optional support payment by mistake, were charged twice, or simply changed your mind, contact support within 30 days with the payment-provider receipt. Virel will request a refund through the payment provider unless fraud, a prior chargeback, or another legal restriction prevents it.

Paid supporter licenses

If paid licenses are enabled later, the checkout page will identify the license and price before payment. Unless a longer mandatory period applies, contact support within 14 days to request a refund. Access associated with a refunded license may be revoked.

Consumer rights

This policy does not limit statutory cancellation, refund, warranty, or other consumer rights. Payment-provider processing time and currency conversion can affect when funds appear.

Request a refund

Use the support page and include the transaction or receipt identifier. Never send a full payment-card number.
