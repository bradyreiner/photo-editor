Support

Installation and purchase support

Start with the checks below. If the problem continues, contact the support address configured for this release.

Purchases and receipts

Use Lemon Squeezy My Orders to recover your receipt and downloads using your purchase email. Keep your receipt as proof of your license. If a purchase or refund needs help, contact support with the order identifier. Never send payment-card numbers.

Subscriptions and device access

Manage your subscription through the configured billing portal to change payment details or cancel renewal. In Looksy, open Preferences → Account to activate a purchase, release an old device, or recover access on this computer. Do not send license keys to support; provide your order identifier.

Installation

  • Download only from the official Looksy page.
  • On macOS, confirm the DMG and app identify a verified Looksy developer and that Gatekeeper does not report damage.
  • On Windows, use Looksy.appinstaller when available so update checks remain registered.

Updates

  • macOS: choose Looksy → Check for Updates.
  • Windows: reopen the App Installer entry or launch the app to trigger the configured check.
  • Never replace an installed app with an older catalog-incompatible build without restoring a matching backup.

Create a support bundle

  1. Open Preferences → Privacy & Support.
  2. Select Create Support Bundle.
  3. Review the generated folder. Logs can contain local file paths but do not include photo bytes.
  4. Attach only the reviewed files relevant to the request.

Include in a report

Include the Looksy version, operating-system version, CPU architecture, what you expected, what happened, and exact reproduction steps. Do not send original client photos without permission.

Security issues

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately. Do not publish exploit details before a fix is available.
