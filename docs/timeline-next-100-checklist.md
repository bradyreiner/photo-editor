# Timeline Next 100 Checklist

This is the next capability pass after the completed 100-item [Timeline UI/UX Remediation Checklist](timeline-ui-ux-remediation-checklist.md). It continues numbering at 101 so the two documents form one auditable 200-item program.

The first pass made the Timeline coherent, responsive, visually polished, and usable for a complete MVP edit. This pass makes it substantially faster and more capable for professional and social-video work without filling the default workspace with permanent controls.

## Product goal

Make Framehouse's Timeline feel calm for a simple cut and exceptionally deep when the task requires it. Common actions should remain direct; advanced editing, audio, captions, effects, review, and delivery should appear through contextual inspectors, focused icon menus, command search, and modular workspaces.

## Scope and completion rules

- These are items 101–200. They do not reopen or restate the completed visual-remediation items 1–100.
- Keep every item unchecked until the behavior, persistence, undo/redo, preview/export truth, and applicable accessibility and performance proof pass.
- Do not expose placeholder controls. If the engine cannot preview, save, reopen, and export a feature truthfully, keep it unavailable.
- Keep the calm core visible: media, save state, transport, active tool, contextual split, and zoom. Put deeper features one predictable action away.
- Prefer focused panels and workspace modes over adding rows to the permanent toolbar.
- **P0** completes a serious editing workflow, **P1** materially improves daily speed or quality, and **P2** adds valuable depth after the core path is reliable.
