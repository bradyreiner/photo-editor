## Retained repair and coverage

`DepthMotionController` now coalesces source replacement by generation without waiting on the GUI thread. Its timeout belongs to the active generation, including repeated inspection of the same file. Worker cancellation deadlines are generation guarded. Application-owned QProcesses retain temporary scripts/request files while terminate/kill and process-group cleanup complete; `aboutToQuit` drains adopted children. Expired group IDs are never signalled later, avoiding stale PGID reuse. Normal application teardown has a bounded fallback.

The expanded fixture uses private fake media/runtime executables and real child processes. It covers rapid source replacement, same-path timeout races, responsive and stubborn workers, repeated cancellation, a parent exiting before its child, temporary-file lifetime, Qt ownership and shutdown. The original implementation fails lifecycle invariants; the repaired implementation passes. Receipts: `ai-lifecycle/final-baseline.json` and `ai-lifecycle/candidate1/functional-final.json`.

A single busy-host diagnostic showed the destructor returning in56µs rather than3,002,192µs, the child group exiting after~253ms, and equal15,482,880-byte peak RSS. These are functional-test observations, not a completed statistical latency/p95 qualification. No real model/inference or physical presentation claim is made.

Permanent windowless coverage also includes real 96-revision paint history, exact serialized recipes and decoded float tiles through controller undo/redo/clear, all-zone heap/RSS/footprint and store/spill accounting, clipboard size rejection and legacy byte parity, deep JSON compatibility, and a repeatable color/LUT churn driver.
