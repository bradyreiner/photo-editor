### Original-baseline and persistence boundaries

Legacy sources acquire their immutable baseline anchor on their **first original reset**. Opening a source captures cheap file metadata and the normal developed-source policy, without synchronously hashing RAW files or writing sidecars during navigation. Reset verifies the complete source SHA-256 and atomically creates/reuses `original-baselines/v1/<asset-and-source-hash>.json`. Different imported asset identities remain distinct even if their bytes match. Missing, corrupt or unwritable anchors, or a changed source since opening, reject reset without changing the working recipe. This does not reconstruct an undocumented historical import/default policy from an older application version.

Board documents persist the chosen archived or restored result. New-process persistence proof does **not** claim that a new global Undo stack survives restart. Local document payloads are atomic and their small index is rebuildable. Newer local fallback payloads/tombstones take precedence over stale catalog responses after an outage; the complete catalog outage/recovery matrix has not been exercised by the final native journey.

## Remaining implementation and acceptance gaps

1. **In-app agent Board/source lifecycle ownership is unavailable.** `main.cpp` registers Carousel with an unavailable default policy and no mutation owner. Original reset is not in the photo-command allowlist; Return to photo remains a QML workflow. Board-local synthetic IDs are not automatically enrolled by catalog-photo task admission. Existing owned layer/text mutations do not establish Board/source parity. Keep these operations unavailable until a native Board snapshot/restore adapter, strict expected IDs/revisions, source/navigation ownership and task-only reversal are implemented and tested. See [registration](../apps/desktop/src/main.cpp), [photo command owner](../apps/desktop/src/agent_photo_commands.h), and the A01–A04 plan cases.
2. **Origin recovery and old-snapshot actions need follow-up.** Missing/ambiguous origins are safely rejected and their documents preserved. A complete origin-specific relink/verified-legacy-resolution flow is not implemented. The captured pre-Board working snapshot is persisted, but its separately named review/restoration action is not exposed. Returning to the current source and reverting to its original baseline are distinct operations and do not substitute for restoring that older working snapshot.
3. **No complete real-camera RAW journey is qualified.** The Board/original subprocess fixtures are generated raster sources. The real inference source is a JPEG. The combined camera RAW → selection/refinement → text → Boards → final delete/return → original reset/Undo → export → new-process reopen journey remains open, including decoded export/source-byte checks.
4. **Human photographic quality and usability acceptance remain open.** No reviewed sky/hair/skin/fabric quality set or photographer task review closes I05/X01–X04. Native gestures and geometry checks are necessary evidence, not human approval of the workflow or the retouch result.
5. **Full model-backed selection/native inference parity remains separate.** Deterministic fake segmentation can qualify ownership and gestures only. The real LaMa controller run does not substitute for a visible native selection-to-inference production walkthrough.
6. **Remote enforcement and release activation are not claimed.** Local CMake/contract/CI-source changes do not prove remote branch protection, required CI activation, notarization, deployment or release acceptance.
